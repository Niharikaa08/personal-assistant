UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC HANDBOOK & CAPSTONE GUIDELINES (2026)

1. ATTENDANCE POLICY
All undergraduate engineering students must maintain a minimum attendance threshold of 75% in both theory and laboratory courses to be eligible for end-semester examinations. Medical condonation up to 10% requires approval from the Dean of Academics within 7 days of absence.

2. FINAL YEAR CAPSTONE PROJECT EVALUATION
The final-year engineering capstone project carries 12 credits and is evaluated across three milestones:
- Synopsis & Architecture Review (20%): Requires formal approval from the assigned Faculty Advisor.
- Mid-Semester Live Demonstration (30%): Assessed on system architecture, algorithmic novelty, and fault tolerance.
- Final Thesis & Viva-Voce Defense (50%): Evaluated by an external examiner panel.

3. ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
All software implementations must include reproducible setup instructions, automated unit tests, and clear attribution of third-party libraries.
